The date that matters

13 May 2027

The main duties of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 apply from this day: notice, consent, security safeguards, breach notification, retention and erasure, people's rights and grievance handling.

Source: commencement notification G.S.R. 843(E), clause (c), and DPDP Rules 2025, rule 1(4). Counted from Gazette publication on 13 November 2025; your lawyer should confirm the exact day.

Maximum penalties in the Act

  • Up to Rs 250 crore for failing to take reasonable security safeguards
  • Up to Rs 200 crore for failing to notify a personal data breach
  • Up to Rs 50 crore for other breaches of the Act or Rules

Source: DPDP Act 2023, Schedule, items 1, 2 and 7. The Data Protection Board decides the amount after an inquiry.

Two products, one workspace

Compliance Kit

For the DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025. First profile: law firms.

  • Data inventory for client files, KYC documents, employee data, visitor records and vendor contracts
  • Readiness score and a prioritised gap list
  • Every gap linked to its section or rule and the source text
  • Six draft documents as PDF: privacy notice, consent text, retention schedule, breach response plan, processor contract checklist, grievance procedure
  • Deadline tracker

Cyber Health Check

Mapped to the CERT-In Directions of 28 April 2022 and CERT-In's baseline controls for MSMEs.

  • Short questionnaire: backups, passwords and MFA, patching, email security, staff training, incident plan
  • Passive checks of your own domain: SPF, DKIM, DMARC, TLS certificate, security headers
  • No port scanning. No intrusive testing
  • Fixes ranked easiest first, with a PDF report

Three promises

01

No invented law

Every scored rule cites a primary source. If we could not verify a rule, it is marked unverified and left out of the score.

02

Your data stays with you

Everything is stored on the machine that runs the suite. No analytics. No third-party scripts.

03

Honest about limits

It is a readiness tool, not a certificate. Every document is a draft for a qualified lawyer to review.

Connect your AI assistant (MCP)

Shield Suite includes a Model Context Protocol server. It runs on your own machine, so your assistant can assess a workspace without your data going to a Wolftech server.

Set-up

Add this to your MCP client's configuration, with the paths for your machine:

{
  "mcpServers": {
    "wolftech-shield-suite": {
      "command": "/path/to/.venv/bin/python",
      "args": ["/path/to/mcp_server.py"]
    }
  }
}

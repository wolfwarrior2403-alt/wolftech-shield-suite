Why this matters

Under the DPDP Act the firm stays responsible for client data it shares with AI vendors (Act s.8(1), s.8(2)), and must protect it with reasonable safeguards (Act s.8(5); Rules r.6). Masking before sharing means the vendor never holds the real data at all.

Masking catches names, emails, phone numbers, PAN, Aadhaar, GSTIN, dates of birth and addresses by column name and pattern. Check the masked file before sharing: unusual free-text fields may need a manual look.
